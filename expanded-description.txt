📖 **BIBLE TRIVIA — The Ultimate Bible Quiz Game!**

Step into the greatest story ever told! Bible Trivia is a fast-paced, multiplayer quiz game that puts your knowledge of the Word of God to the test. Whether you're a lifelong Bible scholar, a Sunday school teacher, a youth group leader, or someone just starting their faith journey, there's a question here for you — and a crown waiting at the top of the leaderboard! 👑

🎯 **HOW TO PLAY**
The game is beautifully simple: one global arena, everyone plays together. A question appears on the screen and every player sees it at the exact same moment. You have 20 seconds to pick the right answer from four options. But don't think too long — the faster you answer correctly, the more points you score! First correct answer = maximum points. Slow down, and the points shrink. Miss it, and you get nothing! Every round is a race against the clock AND against every other player in the arena. ⚡

⭐ **SPEED-BASED SCORING**
Think fast! You can earn up to 100 points per question — but every second you hesitate costs you 5 points. The first player to lock in the correct answer takes the crown for that round. It's not just about knowing the Bible; it's about trusting what you know and acting fast! Can you stay calm under pressure and climb to the top?

📜 **100% BIBLE-BASED — FROM THE SGSS BIBLE**
Every single question is crafted from the SGSS Bible — a simplified, easy-English adaptation of the beloved King James Version. That means the questions are faithful to Scripture and easy to understand. And here's the best part: EVERY question comes with its Bible reference (like John 3:16 or Psalm 23) displayed right on screen, so you can look it up and learn as you play. It's not just a game — it's Bible study disguised as fun! ✨

🔄 **ENDLESS FRESH QUESTIONS**
The question bank is never the same twice! New questions are generated automatically every 30 minutes from random chapters across all 66 books of the Bible — Genesis to Revelation, Old Testament to New Testament. From the creation of the world to the miracles of Jesus, from the Psalms of David to the visions of Revelation — hundreds of questions on people, places, parables, miracles, teachings, prophets, and well-known verses. There's always something new to discover, so you can play all day, every day! 🌍
