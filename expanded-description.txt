🕌 Quran Trivia — Fast-Paced Multiplayer Quran Trivia for Discord!

Race the clock with friends in a single global arena where everyone sees the same Quran question at the same time. Every 20 seconds a new question appears — answer fast, answer right, and climb the leaderboard!

✨ FEATURES
• Instant play — no rooms, no setup. Join a voice channel and start the activity. You're in the game immediately.
• One global arena — every player around the world sees the same question at the same moment. Pure, fair competition.
• 20-second question slots — the clock never stops. Questions rotate continuously, day and night.
• Speed-based scoring — the first correct answer in each slot scores the most points. Fast thinkers win!
• Persistent leaderboard — your username, avatar, and score stay in the game even if you leave and come back later. Your progress is never lost.
• 4-answer multiple choice — clean, mobile-friendly buttons that work with Discord's gamepad and touch controls.
• Reference tags on every question — each question shows its surah reference (like "Al-Baqara 2:255") so you can look it up in the Quran and learn as you play.
• Beautiful emerald & gold design — a calm, elegant Islamic-inspired theme that feels at home on Discord.

📖 QUESTION SOURCING
Every question comes from the SGSS Quran — a simplified, easy-English presentation of the highly regarded Sahih International translation. All 114 surahs are in rotation, from Al-Faatiha to An-Naas. Questions cover:
• Allah's names and attributes (Ar-Rahmaan, Al-Kareem…)
• The prophets (Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, Muhammad ﷺ…)
• People and stories (Yusuf and his brothers, the People of the Cave, Maryam…)
• Places (Makkah, Madinah, Egypt, Jerusalem…)
• Teachings and guidance (prayer, charity, patience, justice…)
• Well-known verses and themes

🆕 FRESH QUESTIONS, ALWAYS
A question-generation pipeline (GitHub Actions + AI) tops up the bank every 30 minutes, sourcing fresh passages from the SGSS Quran. The game never runs out, and repeats are minimized — there's always something new to learn.

🌍 FOR THE MUSLIM COMMUNITY
Quran Trivia was built specifically for Muslim users on Discord who want a fun, respectful way to test and grow their Quran knowledge. All content is kept accurate, respectful, and family-friendly.
